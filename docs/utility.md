A few small tools that don't belong to a bigger feature.

## Polls

```
!poll Best colour? | Red | Green | Blue
```

The bot posts the question with a numbered option for each answer and adds the number reactions, so members vote with one tap. A poll takes two to nine options.

## Information

`!userinfo @sam` shows a member's id, whether they are a bot and their roles. `!communityinfo` shows the member, channel and role counts.

## Analytics

`!analytics` shows messages, joins and leaves per day for the last week, and the busiest channels. Pass a number of days, up to 30, for a longer view: `!analytics 30`. The [dashboard](dashboard.md) draws the same numbers as a chart on its overview page.
